# Research question

Can a wearable, tensegrity-inspired upper-limb system use IMU feedback to produce controlled cable actuation and measurable elbow assistance while remaining lightweight and compliant?

The project combines an inherited rod-and-spring structure with redesigned mechanical components, custom electronics, motor-driven cables, and dual-IMU feedback. Exploratory EMG and motion recordings assess the prototype, with measured limitations retained alongside the results.

Current evidence supports integrated sensing and controller activity. Effective physical assistance and reduced muscle effort remain unproven. Future experiments should separate voluntary motion from delivered motor assistance and verify sensing, force, and synchronized acquisition.
